Good to know

A few questions.

How would a real service confirm the price?

A real cleaning business would review your space, the service requested, and any extra needs, then confirm a quote before booking. This concept does not offer actual cleaning or take payments.

Can this design work for a different business?

Yes. Its structure can be adapted to a local service, independent professional, or small studio: introduce the offer, explain the services, and make the next step clear.

Is the request builder connected to a booking system?

No. It creates a sample request on this page so you can try the interaction. It does not send data, store a booking, or collect contact details.
